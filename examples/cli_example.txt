Available CLI commands:

gen-key
encrypt
decrypt

Usage examples

First generate key pairs:
qcrypto gen-key --alg kyber768 --public pub.key --private priv.key

Then to encrypt file:
qcrypto encrypt --pub pub.key --in notes.txt --out notes.enc

And finally decrypt file:
qcrypto decrypt --key priv.key --in notes.enc --out notes.dec.txt
